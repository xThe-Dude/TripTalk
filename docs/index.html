How we approach it

Four principles that hold the whole thing together.

TripTalk isn’t a recommendation to use anything. It’s a recognition that good information saves lives, and that the people most at risk are the ones with the least access to it.

  1. 01

    Test what you take

    Reagent and fentanyl testing turns the single biggest unknown into a known. We tell you how, plainly.

  2. 02

    Start low, go slow

    Honest onset and duration data so dosing is a deliberate decision, never a guess.

  3. 03

    Set and setting

    A trusted sitter, a safe space, and a clear mind change outcomes more than any substance choice.

  4. 04

    Know the exits

    Interactions, warning signs, and crisis lines — surfaced before they’re ever needed.
