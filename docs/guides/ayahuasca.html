Ayahuasca

Ayahuasca contains MAOIs, which means its single largest danger is interaction — with SSRIs and other antidepressants, with stimulants, and with tyramine-rich foods. The traditional dieta is not superstition; it is a harm-reduction protocol developed over generations. Skipping it can be life-threatening.

The brews below are described so that the interaction risks are unmistakable. If you take any psychiatric medication, ayahuasca can be fatal. Preparation, screening, and an experienced sober facilitator are the whole safety margin.

Safety note Ayahuasca contains MAOIs with serious, potentially fatal interactions with many medications (especially SSRIs) and foods. Medical screening is essential. This is not a recommendation to use.

The traditional Amazonian ayahuasca brew combining Banisteriopsis caapi vine with Psychotria viridis (Chacruna) leaves. This is the classic recipe used by indigenous healers, producing deeply spiritual visions, emotional catharsis, and often profound life insights.
