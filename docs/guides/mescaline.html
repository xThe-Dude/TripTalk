Mescaline

Mescaline experiences are long — often ten to fourteen hours — and that length is itself a safety consideration. Clearing the day, the setting, and the people around you is not optional; it is the experience. Peyote in particular is a conservation concern and sacred to Indigenous communities.

The cacti below are described for awareness. A long arc means nausea, fatigue, and emotional intensity all have to be planned for. Start low, hydrate, and have a sober sitter present for the duration.

Safety note Mescaline has a long duration and significant cardiovascular load; it is contraindicated with heart conditions and certain medications. Peyote is endangered and culturally protected.
