Psilocybin

Psilocybin is the best-studied classical psychedelic, and the most widely documented in this index. That does not make it predictable. The same species can feel gentle at one dose and overwhelming at another, and the difference between a grounding experience and a frightening one is usually set, setting, and dose — not the mushroom.

The varieties below are described for awareness, not encouragement. We list effects, onset, and duration so that anyone choosing to explore can do so deliberately, with realistic expectations and an exit plan. Test what you take, start low, and never dose alone without a sober, trusted sitter.

Safety note Psilocybin can intensify anxiety and is contraindicated with a personal or family history of psychosis, and with certain medications. None of the entries below are a recommendation to use.
